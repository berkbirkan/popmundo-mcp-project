## Writing Songs and Building a Repertoire

Songs lie at the heart of music, and this is also the case in Popmundo. Although you have the option of using cover songs at the beginning of your career, you cannot reach the level of competing in major competitions without writing your own songs. Even songs you buy from the market will pale beside songs you write yourself. If we need to rank them:

Your own song > Song bought from the market > Cover, from higher to lower quality.

## Repertoire

The repertoire is one of the sections you will visit most frequently throughout your career, where you can see the new and old songs your band owns and sort them by criteria such as rehearsal level, quality, popularity, etc.

Using the repertoire features, you can manage your songs by cross-sorting them through two tabs by age, music genre, popularity, fame, rehearsal, quality, stage impact, and musical subgenre.
