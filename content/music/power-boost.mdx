title: "Power Boost"
description: "Power Boost — Music Guide"
---

Some efforts you make for your career come back to you as power boost. Power boost increases by a standard 1% every day. Depending on the level of your concert and the audience size (concerts with very small audiences do not contribute to your power boost), you can receive up to 15% power boost. After your career settles into a routine, increases from concerts will remain at around 3%.

The power boost increase from records depends on the record’s quality and timing. Records released one after another either do not increase power boost at all or provide a very, very small increase. A single released on time provides approximately 55%, and an album 100%, power boost increase.

You can use power boost in 3 ways:

Boost audience enjoyment: By adding it to your stage plan from the general tricks section, you can raise audience enjoyment to the SPLENDID (25) level. If audience enjoyment reaches this value toward the end of the concert, you can place it near the end.

If your songs receive very high values, you can use it at the very beginning of the concert and get the audience excited from the start. You can find the amount that will be spent from your power boost by subtracting the audience enjoyment at the moment the trick is used from the SPLENDID (25) level.

For example, let audience enjoyment be at the terrific (18) level when you perform the boost enjoyment trick. 25−18=7. Each level spends approximately 5% power boost. 7×5=35. There will be an approximately 35% decrease in your power boost. Your power boost does not need to be completely full to use the boost enjoyment trick at your concerts.

Boost repertoire: Raises the rehearsal levels of all songs in your repertoire to 100%. Songs with no musical subgenre selected also have their rehearsals raised to 100%. When starting a new tour, you can use your power boost instead of rehearsing dozens of songs. You need 100% power boost, and all of it is spent.

Boost ambition level: When you enter a competition in your career or prepare to give a high-level concert, by boosting your ambition level you can gain an increase of up to 3–4 levels depending on the situation. When you boost your ambition level, you perform your next concert with this ambition. Your health does not experience much of a decline in the concert for which you boosted ambition. You need 100% power boost, and all of it is spent.
