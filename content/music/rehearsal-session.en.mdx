title: "Rehearsal Session"
description: "Rehearsal Session — Music Guide"
---

To get efficiency from the songs we have added to the repertoire, we need to arrange a rehearsal session and raise their practice level. To arrange a session, you can use Schedule > Arrange Rehearsal Session or the booking assistant. Rehearsal sessions last approximately 4 hours; they finish sooner, but unless something goes wrong with the game engines, they do not exceed 4 hours. For a rehearsal session to succeed, at least 50% of band members must attend. The remaining 50% can wander around here and there; no big problem :p

You can rehearse at most 10 songs at once. You can choose your songs until the rehearsal session ends. If you do not choose a subgenre specific to your music genre for the songs you will rehearse, you cannot raise the rehearsal level above a certain percentage.

Playing songs at concerts also increases their rehearsal by around 2 percentage points. Rather than arranging a session and lowering your mood for a song whose rehearsal remains at a level such as 95–96, its rehearsal will already be complete after 2–3 concerts.

Your success in a rehearsal session depends, from most to least, on your music genre skill, how many songs you rehearse at once, how many band members attend the rehearsal, and the quality of the venue where you rehearse.
