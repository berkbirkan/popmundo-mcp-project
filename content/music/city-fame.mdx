title: "City Fame"
description: "City Fame — Music Guide"
---

The fundamental value to take into account for your ticket sales and record sales is your city fame. Also, the position of your fame average in the global ranking directly affects your band’s ranking. There are two factors that directly increase city fame: concerts and media power. Many other factors are reflected in your city fame through media power.

Your city fame tends to decline over time. After 42 days have passed since your last visit, this decline becomes noticeable, and after 56 days, sharp declines begin. Canceling or missing a concert directly lowers your city fame.
