To introduce the songs you have added to your repertoire and fully rehearsed to the audience, next comes arranging and giving a concert. To play in a bar, you can use the booking assistant, or, as the more preferred method, send a message to bar chain owners and ask for invitations in accordance with your tour plan, if you have one. You can reach bar chain owners through music genre topics in the general forums or through the company forum. Apart from bars, you can also give concerts in stadiums; we will discuss this separately.

Your ticket limit for a concert in a bar is 10,000. If no tickets at all are sold for the concert you arranged, unfortunately your concert fails and receives a much lower score than it should. Therefore, you should arrange your concerts in advance in a planned way. Ticket prices and your average city fame also affect your ticket sales, with fame in that city having the greatest effect. A low ticket price does not directly increase your ticket sales; it only increases their speed. Its indirect effect is that you sell more tickets from a high level before your city fame starts to decline. Your tickets will sell much faster in a bar of your own music genre. Experienced bar owners will help you with ticket pricing appropriate to your fame level. Still, it is useful to check your ticket sales from time to time. I will discuss other concert details in the tour plan and stage plan sections.

If you cannot make it to a concert, you should cancel it. Your fame will drop sharply according to the number of tickets sold, but will rise again over time. To make up for this drop, you should give a concert shortly afterwards. If you happen to miss the concert, this time, because the average of the concerts within that year is taken, a zero-point concert will pull down the average and your fame will remain stuck at a certain level that year. Best of all is to miss no concerts.
