There are 17 different music genres you can choose to start your career. The choice here can mostly be made in line with your understanding of role-playing. In a crowded music genre, it is harder to enter the radio charts and climb to the top of the charts. This can set your career back somewhat compared to artists in less crowded music genres. Apart from that, there is theoretically no difference between making Latin music and dancing, and making rock music and smashing instruments.
