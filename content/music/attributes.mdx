Experience points are a rookie’s most valuable treasure, a guide from knowledge. Because these points, which come in abundance at the beginning, will become harder to earn later. For a successful music career, I recommend developing all your personal attributes as much as possible in the following order.

- Intelligence: It will provide significant benefits during the skill development process and later in stage tricks.
- Musical Ability: The fundamental elements of music, such as composing songs and recording songs, gain value by developing this attribute.
- Charm: It affects stage tricks, the performance score received on stage, and talking to the media.
- Looks: It affects stage tricks and the performance score received on stage. It is also the most important attribute for music video shoots.
- Voice: It affects stage tricks and the performance score received on stage. It has absolutely no connection to the lead vocals instrument skill!
- Constitution: An attribute I think is useful for keeping your health high.

That is all! Exactly 480 experience points. I suggest starting your calculations now: if 3 come weekly, 2 from quests, I celebrate my birthday, and a few from zombies.
