At the very end of our guide is Popmundo’s most prestigious competition, the Popmundo Festival. The Festival is organized approximately once every 4–5 Popmundo years on dates determined by the game developers. It is a two-stage competition. As a result of the qualifying round held in 10 cities, also determined by the developers, a total of 30 bands giving the top 3 performances in each city, and, apart from these bands, the top 5 of the best-performing bands, earn the right to give concerts in the finals. The qualifying round lasts 3 days.

There is a gap of approximately 10 days between the qualifying round and the final round. Afterwards, the 3-day final round begins. All bands that qualify to play in the final receive the “I played at the Festival” achievement. Placement awards are also given to the top 3 bands. You can access detailed information about the Festival from the Popmundo Festival heading in the Social menu.
