title: "Saint Kobe’s Day"
description: "Saint Kobe’s Day — Music Guide"
---

The 40th day of every Popmundo year is celebrated as Saint Kobe’s Day. The feature of this day is that, if you can successfully complete one of the trails you choose in 4 cities, you are entitled to receive 1* in a music genre skill of your choice. You can enter 1 trail per year. By entering the trail again and again every year, you can increase the star level of different music genre skills without developing skills.

You can access detailed information about Saint Kobe’s Day trails and maps here: 2241740.1
