description: "Extra Services — Music Guide"
---

Before a concert, every artist, modest or spoiled, has certain requests from the venue where they will perform. These are called extra services, and you can find them in the extra services section on the artist page. When requested correctly, extra services contribute a certain mood increase. Requesting too few or too many can also lower your mood. The cost of extra services is paid after the concert by the venue where you performed.

An easy method for determining extra services is that you can cheat by looking at a band whose fame level is similar to yours and which is satisfied with its extra services. Or you can find the service range appropriate for you by adding and removing them one by one. As your fame level increases, your demand for extra services and the amount requested for the same service will increase. Extra services have no effect on your concert level.
