title: "Cover Songs"
description: "Cover Songs — Music Guide"
---

At the beginning of your career, if you have not yet written your own songs and buying songs from the market is not an option for you, cover songs are great as a starting point. In short, covering means other artists playing on stage songs that have been released or recorded on other artists’ albums and singles.

To add cover songs to your repertoire, you can open the radio charts in the rankings section and choose songs currently near the top of the charts or songs that will reach the top within a week or two. What you need to pay attention to is that the songs you choose are in the same music genre as yours and that the dominant instruments match your stage roles as much as possible. A good cover song should have fame that has not yet turned yellow, have managed to enter the radio charts, and have popularity on an upward curve.
