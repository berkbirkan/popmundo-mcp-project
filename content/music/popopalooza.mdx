---
title: "Popopalooza"
description: "Popopalooza — Music Guide"
---

It is a competition that starts on the 13th day of the Popmundo calendar and lasts approximately 9–10 days or 9–10 rounds. No criteria are required to participate in Popopalooza. Starting from the first day of each year, you can register through Social > Popopalooza.

The competition works as follows: participating artists can give concerts at venues of their choice. Until 00:00 CET, the most successful concert they have given within that day is entered into the ranking. In every round, the best 50% of bands that give concerts advance to the next round. The competition continues this way until only one artist remains at the end.

Because those who make it beyond the fifth round of Popopalooza earn an achievement, many bands boost their ambition level. Also, bands coming from the Big Bang event with a high-quality repertoire will make things difficult for Popopalooza competitors in the first 5–6 rounds.

Achievements are distributed to those who pass the fifth round in Popopalooza, the band that comes first in the final round, and one (or two bands) eliminated in the final round. You can access detailed information about Popopalooza from the Popopalooza heading in the Social menu.

Similar to the Big Bang competition, stadium concerts are arranged starting before the event to try to raise song popularities to the maximum level.
