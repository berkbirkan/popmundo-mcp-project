Hello. I am here again to update the “Career Guide” project I made years ago and to create a guide for those who will enter the music industry, the most enjoyable part of Popmundo, those looking for advice, those who will compare their knowledge and say, “Actually, that is not how it works,” and the tiny little second generations. This guide, which I prepared based on the information I have acquired, my experiences, and what I have learned, is never 100% correct. I may have omissions and even incorrect information. You can always send a message about corrections. I wish everyone a wonderful music career.

“A truly breathtaking guide, practically a bedside reference.” 10/10 – The Insider
