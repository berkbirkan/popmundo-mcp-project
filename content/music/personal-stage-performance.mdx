Independent of the songs played at a concert, each band member also has an individual performance value, and it directly affects the concert result. We can list the factors affecting the score you receive on stage as follows:

- Stage role skills (skills in the first and second roles)
- Having the instruments for your stage roles
- Health and mood
- Looks, charm, and voice attributes, in order of importance
- Basic Sex Appeal
- Instrument condition (you can increase it by tuning and polishing)
- Instrument history (star value)
- Age

In addition to the above, how busy your schedule is also affects your stage performance. If you perform too many concerts, record, and rehearse consecutively, your stage performance may drop. When you meet all the criteria completely, you generally receive a 4.5* rating on stage. If you have a busy schedule, it drops to 4*. You rarely give a 5* performance. At a boosted ambition level, the performance value you receive on stage also increases.
