title: "Groupies and Fans"
description: "Groupies and Fans — Music Guide"
---

Your fans who love you line up backstage after a concert, sometimes to meet you and sometimes to do crazy things together. From the settings on your character page, you can decide who you want to show interest in you after concerts. To be intimate with and spend time with your fans, your city fame needs to be around decent (10) or slightly above.

Spending time with your fans contributes 1–2% to your personal fame. However, as a result of this adventure, you may catch gonorrhea. When you rent a private jet, if you select “Allow groupies to hang out,” you can join the Mile High club, that is, the club of those who are intimate on a plane. This similarly increases your personal fame.

If groupies do not come after a concert despite everything being as it should, check whether there are as many personal assistants in your crew as people in your band. Also, groupies are affected by sex addiction and the newly added locked chastity thing.
