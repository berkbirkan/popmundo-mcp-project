description: "Scene Rehearsal — Music Guide"
---

If we want the first songs of the singles we release to enter the charts and become more popular, the way to do this is to make a video. Before making a video, you also need to fully rehearse the scene. If you wish, after recording the song for which you will make a video, you can create your video through Artist > Videos, or start rehearsal for any recorded song you have. Before the shoot, you can change the song being rehearsed without affecting the rehearsal amounts. This way, you can get your rehearsal out of the way at the first opportunity without waiting for the recording of the song for which the video will be made.

Afterwards, choosing scene rehearsal from your focuses and selecting the scene you will rehearse will be enough. With increases of 18–21% per update, our scene rehearsal will finish in 5–6 updates. If you start two scene rehearsals at the same time, your scene rehearsal amounts decline over time. Therefore, I do not recommend starting to rehearse the scene for another video before shooting one.

While rehearsing the scene, you should also determine a director for the video. If the director is within the band, rehearsing only as an actor is enough. They will receive points as both an actor and director in the video. If someone from outside the band directs, you must invite them for scene rehearsal and have them rehearse like the actors.
