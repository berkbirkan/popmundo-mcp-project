Another element affecting your market shares is television shows. Alongside your market share, they also contribute somewhat to the popularity of your latest existing song with a video and to your personal fame. Invitations are sent 2 to 4 weeks before your concerts, from television stations near the bars where you give concerts. Concerts selling very few tickets may not be taken into account by television stations.

Although invitations are sent in accordance with your tour plan, many invitations will also come at the same time as your concert, in a different city, or in ways you cannot make it to. You should choose and accept those you can attend without making your schedule difficult. You cannot have more than four accepted or not-yet-accepted television show invitations at the same time, so do not forget to reject invitations that do not interest you. Invitations you do not answer will disappear within a week. Television shows generally last one hour, but can sometimes last a little longer. Make sure you do not accept television show invitations that overlap with other activities you have planned.
