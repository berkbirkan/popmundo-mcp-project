Before moving on to singles and albums, we need a studio that can release these records. You can find many producers working with successful artists both through the forums and through Rankings > Highest-Quality Venues (recording studios). The greatest contribution a producer can give you will be their experience and knowledge of music. It is also in your interest to choose a producer who is active enough to release your records and videos on time and not create stock problems.

There is one more thing your producer can give you: advertising level. The advertising service, consisting of 5 levels, acts exactly like a parachute that slows the decline of your city fame; you do not see sharp drops. The quality difference between advertising levels increases with cumulatively diminishing returns. In other words, while there is a very big difference between levels 1 and 2, there is very little difference between levels 4 and 5. For this reason, level 5 advertising should only be provided when you have nowhere else to spend your money.
