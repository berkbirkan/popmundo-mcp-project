description: "Personal Fame — Music Guide"
---

The popularity you gain from your music career, the profession you have, and the people you hang out with is called personal fame. It is also commonly known as star quality (SQ). The factors increasing your personal fame are:

- Dating or having sex with people with high personal fame
- Your ranking in your music career
- Fame increases from groupies and your fans
- Increases from television shows
- Increases from certain special objects and foods
- Your profession (professions that are not exclusive to VIP users have a negative effect on your personal fame.

The greatest contribution of your personal fame to your career is its effect on your video performance. Also, if your personal fame is 50% or above, you can use a stage name. After setting a stage name once, you can use it even if your fame falls below 50%.
