Unlike bars, the audience upper limit in stadiums is 35,000. Attracting this many spectators to a stadium is difficult even for the most famous bands, so do not be sad if you cannot achieve full capacity. However, there is a consequence to this. The fewer tickets you sell in stadiums, the more negatively your concert will be affected. Therefore, if you cannot exceed the threshold of selling 20,000 tickets, the total of two bar concerts, a stadium concert may do you more harm than good.

Ticket prices and your average city fame also affect your ticket sales, with fame in that city having the greatest effect. A low ticket price does not directly increase your ticket sales; it only increases their speed. Its indirect effect is that you sell more tickets from a high level before your city fame starts to decline.

The contribution of stadium concerts to your career is providing a large popularity increase depending on the number of spectators. While you can gain 4–5% popularity by giving two bar concerts for 10,000 people per day, a stadium concert for 30,000 people brings 6–7%. You should arrange your stadium concerts as the first concert of the day; otherwise, as the second concert of the day, you cannot get the efficiency you expect from the popularity increase. My advice is to arrange your stadium concerts as the only concert of the day.

You can arrange at most 10 stadium concerts within one Popmundo year. In other words, you can arrange a total of 20 stadium concerts consecutively in the last ten days of year 100 and the first ten days of year 101. This is of great importance for the Big Bang competition, which takes place on the ninth day of the year. I will discuss the use of stadiums in the competitions section.
