To raise your songs’ popularity a little above the levels reached by concerts and get your song into the radio charts, you need to release a video. You can arrange a shoot for a video whose scene rehearsal you have completed. Arrange a shoot for the video from the screen where you view video rehearsals. The quality of the venue you arrange is quite important for the quality of the shoots. From the rankings section, you can view the highest-quality parks globally and arrange your shoot accordingly.

A video shoot lasts approximately 3 hours 45 minutes. You should use your health-, fame-, or mood-boosting items and your makeup kit before the video finishes. If you use them toward the end of this period, you minimize the health or fame decline from an update you receive.

If you are not satisfied with the quality of your shoot, you can simply arrange another video shoot in the same way without damaging your rehearsals. Many bands comfortably shoot videos at EPIC level, so do not hesitate to try if you believe you can do better.

Videos are released every 28 days just like singles, and depending on your strategy, you can release them together with the single. If you raise the song you shot to 35–40% popularity before the video is released, you get maximum efficiency from the radio charts and the popularity increase. If you do not release the video you shot within 14 days, its score starts to drop. Therefore, shooting close to the release date is to your benefit. Videos are affected by release timing more than records. As much as possible, do not release your video early.

Factors effective in video shoots:

- Basic Acting skill
- Your looks (found in the attributes section)
- Your rehearsal level
- The quality and condition of the venue where the shoot takes place
- Your personal fame
- Your health and mood
- Your director’s health, mood, and skills (Basic Photography, Cinematography, your music genre skill)
