Popmundo is a deep blue planet made up of 49 cities, with a calendar year lasting 56 days. Artists travel from city to city giving concerts throughout the year to increase their fame, climb the charts and achieve certain achievements, and earn income from single and album sales. Within this busy pace, they also make time for special days and nights, hunting events, and piracy on the seven seas. You can find the expanded explanation of this summary below.
