description: "Song Market — Music Guide"

Apart from writing our songs and covering, another option is buying and using songs that others have put up for sale through the song market. You can access these lists through Artist > Song Market > Songs. Generally, most songs offered for sale consist of songs that family members write and sell among themselves to support each other. You can post an advertisement on the forum to have songs written for you, or ask your friends if you have them. Market songs are not indispensable.

A purchased song of 24–24 quality is equivalent to your own song of approximately 21–22 or 22–22 quality. In other words, if you can write your own songs and build your repertoire from them, you will not miss market songs in terms of quality. You can make videos for hit songs you buy from the market and use them as the first song of your single.
